Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account dashboard. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan includes a 7-day free trial with full access to course materials. No credit card required to start.

Do you offer refunds?

If you are not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact our support team for a full refund — no questions asked.

Are payment plans available?

Pro and Team plans can be split into monthly installments. Reach out via our contact page and we will set up a plan that works for you.
